A particle starts on the middle site of a chain. With a clean lattice it spreads at a steady speed, so the cloud grows linearly in time. Give every site a random energy and it stops: the wavefunction freezes with an exponential tail, in one dimension for any amount of disorder. The picture shows |ψ|² (colour, log scale) against time running downwards. Every step is a 4th-order Runge-Kutta step of the Schrödinger equation in the QMC library (anderson_evolve), running in your browser.
