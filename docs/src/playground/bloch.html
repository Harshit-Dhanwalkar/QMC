QMC Playground - Bloch sphere

One qubit, one arrow. Drive it with a resonant or detuned field and it precesses about the amber axis b = (Ω, 0, Δ); let it decay and the arrow shrinks into the ball as the state becomes mixed. Every step is a 4th-order Runge-Kutta step of the Lindblad equation in the QMC library (bloch_evolve), running in your browser. Drag the sphere to rotate.
