QMC Playground — Ising quench

Prepare 256 spins in the ground state of a transverse-field Ising chain, then suddenly change the field from hi to hf. Correlations between distant spins spread through the chain inside a light cone. When the quench crosses the critical point h = J, the probability to return to the initial state (the Loschmidt echo) vanishes at isolated times and its rate function has kinks: dynamical quantum phase transitions. The chain is solved exactly with free fermions by the QMC library (tfim_quench_zz), so every number below is exact, with no truncation.

H = −JΣ σzjσzj+1 − hΣ σxj, J = 1. Each momentum pair (k, −k) is a spin-1/2 that precesses about the post-quench field; correlations are Pfaffians of Fourier sums of those spins, the echo is the product of the pair overlaps, L(t) = Πk[1 − sin²φk sin²(εkt)]. The right plot shows the quasiparticle density created in each mode; a quench across h = J puts exactly one half in the critical mode k*, which gives the kinks at t*n = π(n+½)/εk*. The front of the cone moves at 4 min(J, hf). Source: physics/tfim_quench.c, wrapper wasm/qmc_tfim.c; magnetisation, echo and correlations are checked against exact diagonalisation in tests/test_tfim_quench.c.
