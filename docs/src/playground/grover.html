QMC Playground - Grover search

Each cell is one item of an unsorted database; its colour is that item's quantum amplitude. Click cells to mark the ones you are looking for, then step the algorithm: the oracle flips the sign of the marked amplitudes and the diffusion step inverts every amplitude about the mean, which pumps amplitude into the marked items. The state rotates by 2θ per step with sinθ = √(M/N), so the success probability is sin²((2k+1)θ): it peaks near (π/4)√(N/M) steps and then falls again. The whole state vector is simulated live by the QMC library (grover_multi_step).

One iteration is G = (2|s⟩⟨s| − 1)·O with O flipping the sign of the marked amplitudes. G acts as a rotation by 2θ in the plane spanned by the uniform marked and unmarked states (the small circle shows the state angle (2k+1)θ), which is why more iterations are not always better. With M = N/4 one step is enough; with M = N/2 the algorithm cannot move at all, so the page refuses to mark half the items. Source: physics/grover_multi.c, wrapper wasm/qmc_grover.c; the state vector is checked against the closed form in tests/test_grover_multi.c.
