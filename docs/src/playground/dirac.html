A relativistic electron (two-component spinor) meets a sharp potential step. Non-relativistically, a step higher than the electron's energy reflects it almost completely. Raise the step above E + mc² and the Dirac equation lets the packet through anyway, as a negative-energy state. Each frame is the QMC split-operator solver (dirac_evolve_1d) running in your browser.
