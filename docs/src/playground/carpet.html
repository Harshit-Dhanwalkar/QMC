QMC Playground - Quantum carpet

A Gaussian wave packet bounces around an infinite square well. Its density |ψ(x,t)|² draws the quantum carpet on the left (position across, time downward). Because the energies are En = n², every mode returns to its starting phase at the same time T: at T the packet is exactly back, at T/2 it is its own mirror image, at T/4 it is two copies at once (a cat state). Drag the cursor down the carpet; the plot on the right shows the density at that time and the curve below shows the return probability. The modes and phases are computed live by the QMC library (box_wavefunction).

return probability |⟨ψ0|ψ(t)⟩|² over one revival time
ψ(x,t) = ∑n cn e−i n² t √2 sin(nπx) on the well [0,1], cn the overlap of the Gaussian with each mode, 160 modes kept (the page reports the weight lost to truncation). Exact identities, all checked in tests/test_box_revival.c: ψ(x,T) = ψ0(x), ψ(x,T/2) = −ψ0(1−x), and ψ(x,T/4) = ½[(1−i)ψ0(x) − (1+i)ψ0(1−x)]. The carpet is the same Talbot effect as light behind a diffraction grating. Source: physics/box_revival.c, wrapper wasm/qmc_carpet.c.
