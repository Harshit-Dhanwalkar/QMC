QMC Playground — Aharonov–Bohm ring

Electrons on a ring of lattice sites feel no force from a magnetic flux Φ threaded through its hole, yet every observable depends on it — periodically, with period one flux quantum Φ0. Drag the flux: the energy levels En = −2t cos(2π(n−Φ/Φ0)/N) slide along, the filled ring carries a persistent current, and an electron entering at the left lead takes both arms to the right lead — with a relative phase 2πΦ/Φ0, so the transmission oscillates. Computed by the QMC library (ab_ring.c) with a Green's-function scattering solver.

The eigenstates are plane waves on the ring, so the spectrum is exactly En = −2t cos(2π(n−φ)/N), repeating as n → n−1 when φ → φ+1. The current of ne spinless electrons is −dE/dφ; for odd ne it is diamagnetic at small flux and for even ne paramagnetic. The leads are semi-infinite tight-binding chains with self-energy Σ = (E − i√(4t²−E²))/2; with G = (E − H − Σa − Σb)−1 the transmission is T = Γ²|Gab|² and T + R = 1 is checked in the tests. With the leads on opposite sites the two arms are identical and T vanishes exactly at φ = 1/2. Source: physics/ab_ring.c, wrapper wasm/qmc_ring.c; spectrum, current, unitarity, reciprocity and the half-flux zero are verified in tests/test_ab_ring.c.
