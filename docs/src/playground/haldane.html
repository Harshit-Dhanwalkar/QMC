QMC Playground - Haldane Chern insulator

Graphene with a twist: electrons hop between next-nearest neighbours with a complex amplitude t2e±iφ, which breaks time-reversal symmetry without any net magnetic field, and a sublattice mass M opens a gap. When |M| < 3√3 t2|sinφ| the filled band carries a Chern number ±1 and a ribbon of the material has one-way edge states: one on each side, moving in opposite directions, immune to backscattering. Click in the phase diagram to move through the transition. The ribbon (14 cells wide) is diagonalised live by the QMC library (tb_ribbon_bands).

H = Σ t1 c†icj + t2e±iφ c†icj + M(nA − nB), with t1 = 1. The bulk gap closes at one of the two valleys when |M| = 3√3 t2|sinφ|, which is the boundary drawn in the phase diagram. Click a state in the spectrum to see where it lives across the ribbon: edge states decay into the bulk within a few cells, and the one on the first edge has the opposite velocity to the one on the far edge.
