QMC Playground - Zeeman effect to Paschen-Back

A magnetic field splits the 2P levels of an alkali atom, but how depends on its strength against the spin-orbit coupling ξ. For weak fields each fine-structure level splits evenly by gJmj (the anomalous Zeeman effect, g = 4/3 and 2/3); for strong fields the coupling breaks and the levels fan out as ml + 2ms, so the D lines regroup into the normal Zeeman triplet (Paschen-Back). Drag the field and watch the six levels, the ten allowed lines from 2S, their polarisations and their intensities. H = ξ L·S + μBB(Lz + 2Sz), solved in closed form per mj block by the QMC library (zeeman_full.c).

Each state is |j, mj⟩; mj = ±3/2 stay pure while the two mj = ±1/2 pairs mix (a 2×2 block with off-diagonal ξ/√2). A line from 2S with spin ms into a P state has weight |⟨ml, ms|state⟩|2, because light does not act on spin; at zero field D2 is twice as strong as D1 and in Paschen-Back all intensity sits in three lines at −1, 0, +1 (in units of μBB) plus small ξ offsets. The field in tesla uses μB = 0.46686 cm−1/T and ξ of 0.227 (Li), 11.46 (Na), 38.5 (K), 158 cm−1 (Rb) for the 2P / np level; the slider is in units of ξ. Source: physics/zeeman_full.c, wrapper wasm/qmc_zeeman.c; limits, slopes, sum rules and the block solution are verified against a 6×6 diagonalisation in tests/test_zeeman_full.c.
