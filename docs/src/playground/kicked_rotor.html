QMC Playground - Kicked rotor

Kick a spinning rotor with a force K sin θ once per turn, again and again. For K above about 1 the classical motion is chaotic and the momentum diffuses without limit, ⟨p²⟩ ≈ (K²/2) n. The quantum rotor follows it for a few kicks and then stops: interference freezes the spreading (dynamical localisation, the cousin of Anderson localisation) and leaves exponential tails. Every kick is an FFT split-step in the QMC library (krotor_step), running in your browser.

At ℏ = 4π (resonance) the free rotation does nothing and ⟨m²⟩ = (nK/ℏ)²/2 exactly; this and the diffusion rate are checked in tests/test_kicked_rotor.c.
