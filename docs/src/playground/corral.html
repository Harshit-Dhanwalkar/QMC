QMC Playground - Quantum corral

A particle confined to a circular wall has eigenstates ψ = Jm(kr)·cos(mθ) or sin(mθ), where the wall forces Jm(k) = 0 and the energy is E = k². Modes: step through the spectrum - every m > 0 level is a degenerate cos/sin pair. Packet: click to place a Gaussian, drag to give it momentum, and watch it bounce; its evolution is the exact sum of the modes with phases e−ik²t, computed by the QMC library (circular_billiard.c).

The Dirichlet eigenvalues are the zeros of Bessel functions, jm,n, and the mean level count follows Weyl's law N(k) ≈ k²/4 − k/2 (area term minus a perimeter correction). The packet is projected on the ~150 modes with k < 26, so very fast or very narrow packets lose a little weight (shown as “kept”). Source: physics/circular_billiard.c, wrapper wasm/qmc_corral.c; zeros, orthonormality and nodal structure are verified in tests/test_circular_billiard.c.
