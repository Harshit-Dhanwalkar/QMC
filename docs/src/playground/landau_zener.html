QMC Playground - Landau-Zener

Two energy levels that would cross are pushed apart by a coupling Ω into an avoided crossing. Sweep the detuning through it: slowly, the system follows its level (adiabatic); quickly, it jumps across the gap (diabatic), with probability P = exp(−πΩ²/2v), the Landau-Zener formula. Sweep back and the two paths interfere, giving Stückelberg fringes. Each point is a 4th-order Runge-Kutta integration in the QMC library (lz_sweep), running in your browser.
