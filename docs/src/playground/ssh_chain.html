QMC Playground — SSH chain

A chain of atoms with alternating hoppings v (inside a cell) and w (between cells) is the simplest topological insulator. For w < v it is ordinary. For w > v the bulk gap closes and reopens, the Bloch function winds once around the origin (winding number 1, Zak phase π) and two states appear in the gap, one at each end of the chain. Random hopping cannot move them; a staggered potential can. The 20-cell chain is diagonalised live by the QMC library (ssh_chain_solve), with v = 1.

H = Σ v|Ac⟩⟨Bc| + w|Bc⟩⟨Ac+1| + h.c., bulk bands E(k) = ±|v + w eik|. The right plot is the curve h(k) = v + w eik in the complex plane: it encircles the origin exactly when w > v. Edge-state energy ≈ ±(w²−v²)/w·(v/w)n, decay length ξ = 1/ln(w/v) cells. Source: physics/ssh_chain.c, wrapper wasm/qmc_ssh.c; energy, decay length, winding number, Zak phase and chiral symmetry are checked in tests/test_ssh_chain.c.
