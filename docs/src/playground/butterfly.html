Spectrum of an electron on a square lattice in a magnetic field, as a function of flux α = p/q per plaquette. Each vertical line is one diagonalisation by the QMC tight-binding module (tb_model_hofstadter); the colour of every gap is its Chern number (quantised Hall conductance). Hover or tap to inspect, click a gap to check it numerically.

Band edges come from four special k-points per flux (exact for this model, checked against a dense k-grid in butterfly_check.c); gap labels come from the TKNN Diophantine equation, and clicking a gap recomputes the Chern number with the library's Fukui–Hatsugai–Suzuki routine.
