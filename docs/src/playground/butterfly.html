QMC Playground - Hofstadter butterfly

Spectrum of an electron on a square lattice in a magnetic field, as a function of flux α = p/q per plaquette. Each vertical line is one diagonalisation by the QMC tight-binding module (tb_model_hofstadter); the colour of every gap is its Chern number (quantised Hall conductance). Hover or tap to inspect, click a gap to check it numerically, scroll to zoom, drag to pan.
