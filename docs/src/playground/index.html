QMC Playground - quantum double-slit

A 2D wavepacket solved with the soft_evolve_2d split-operator FFT routine from the QMC C library, compiled to a 21 KB WebAssembly module. Nothing is precomputed - every frame is a live Schrödinger solve.

Detector screen: accumulated probability along the dashed line
Setup:
Drag on the simulation to paint your own potential walls (right-click drag erases). Try making a slit pattern with three or four openings.
