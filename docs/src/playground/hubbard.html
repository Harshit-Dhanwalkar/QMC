QMC Playground - Hubbard dimer

Two sites, two electrons: they hop with amplitude t and pay an energy U whenever they share a site. The model is diagonalised exactly by the QMC library (hubbard_dimer.c), so every curve is the true answer. At U = 0 the electrons share a band orbital; as U/t grows double occupancy is squeezed out, one electron settles on each site, and the pair becomes a Heisenberg singlet with exchange J = 4t²/U - the seed of Mott insulators and antiferromagnets.

The N = 2 spectrum is E = U/2 ± √(U²/4 + 4t²), U and a three-fold triplet at 0, so the singlet-triplet gap closes as 4t²/U. The ground state is a mix of the covalent singlet and the doublon (“ionic”) state; the doublon weight is ½(1 − U/2Δ) and ⟨S1·S2⟩ = −¾×(covalent weight). The entropy is that of the one-site reduced density matrix (charge and spin entanglement between the two sites); the quench starts from |↑,↓⟩, whose staggered moment oscillates as cos(Jt) when U >> t; thermal curves use the six canonical N = 2 states. Source: physics/hubbard_dimer.c, wrapper wasm/qmc_hubbard.c; spectrum, limits, quench and thermal averages are verified in tests/test_hubbard_dimer.c.
